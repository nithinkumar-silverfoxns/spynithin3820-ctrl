# SILVERFOXNS JARVIS — 60-second demo video script

A shot-by-shot script to record once the app is running on your phone. Screen
recording + voiceover; no editing tricks needed. Times are approximate.

**Tagline to open/close with:** *"Your Personal AI. Your Voice. Your Control."*

---

### 0:00–0:06 · Cold open (splash)
- **Shot:** App launches → splash with the glowing ◉ mark and "JARVIS / SILVERFOXNS".
- **VO:** "This is JARVIS, by Silverfoxns — a personal AI that lives on your phone."

### 0:06–0:14 · Secure unlock
- **Shot:** Face ID / fingerprint prompt → dashboard fades in.
- **VO:** "It's locked behind your biometrics, so only you get in."

### 0:14–0:24 · The dashboard
- **Shot:** Slowly pan the dashboard — the animated voice orb pulsing, the live
  status tiles (Assistant: Online, Voice AI: Ready, Security: Encrypted), quick actions.
- **VO:** "One glance shows everything — your assistant, your security, and quick actions."

### 0:24–0:36 · A real command
- **Shot:** Tap the orb → Voice screen. Type or say *"Navigate to the nearest coffee shop."*
  JARVIS replies out loud and the Maps app opens.
- **VO:** "Ask in plain language, and it acts — opening apps, navigation, messages and more."

### 0:36–0:48 · Security in action (the money shot)
- **Shot:** Say *"Call Mom."* → the confirmation banner slides up: **"Do you want me
  to call Mom?"** with Yes / Cancel. Tap **Yes** → the dialer opens.
- **VO:** "Anything sensitive — calls, messages, contacts — always asks first. Nothing
  happens without your say-so."

### 0:48–0:56 · Privacy centre
- **Shot:** Open Privacy & Security → toggle **Conversation memory** on; show the
  "encrypted at rest" and "audit log" bullets.
- **VO:** "You control what it remembers. Everything's encrypted, and your API keys
  never leave your server."

### 0:56–1:00 · Close
- **Shot:** Return to dashboard, orb pulsing. Fade to the ◉ mark + tagline.
- **VO:** "SILVERFOXNS JARVIS. Your Personal AI. Your Voice. Your Control."

---

## Recording tips
- iOS: Control Centre → Screen Recording (enable mic for voiceover).
- Android: Quick Settings → Screen record (turn on microphone).
- Record in **dark mode** — the cinematic palette and the glowing orb read best.
- Keep the backend running and an `OPENAI_API_KEY` set so replies are live.
- Do a silent dry run first so the taps land on the beat of your voiceover.
